# Profile

I am an undergraduate Physics student (B.Sc.) at the São Carlos Institute of Physics, University of São Paulo (IFSC - USP).

## Academic Interests

My primary interest of study lies at the intersection of theoretical physics and some mathematical structures, specifically concerning the following topics:

* Condensed Matter Physics and Phase Transitions;
* Mathematics: Lie Algebras, Chiral and Vertex Algebras, Group Theory, and Representation Theory;
* Applications of algebraic methods to Physics, particularly in Quantum Field Theory (QFT).

## Independent Projects

The repositories hosted on this profile predominantly reflect independent studies and topics explored in my free time, which are not necessarily linked to my main areas of research. These include:

* Computational Physics;
* Cryptography;
* Unix-based operating systems;
* QubesOS and other security-related topics.
